## What is Descriptive Statistics?

Descriptive Statistics is used to summarize and describe the main characteristics of a dataset.

It helps answer questions such as:

- What is the typical value?
- How spread out is the data?
- Where does a particular value stand compared to others?
- What does the overall distribution of the data look like?
